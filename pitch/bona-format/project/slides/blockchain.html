Why blockchain

Six ideas, in plain words.

Smart contract

A program that holds the money and can only follow its rule.

Here: the LayBy contract

Wallet

Each person’s own key. Nobody else can sign for them.

Here: shop and customer each sign

Transaction

Every payment is public, timestamped and final.

Here: a receipt on Snowtrace

Can’t be changed

Once deployed, nobody can edit the rule. Not even us.

Here: no admin, no withdraw

Agreement ID

Every lay-by gets a number, and every payment names it.

Here: TV #1, Fridge #2, Sofa #3

Avalanche

Fast, with fees small enough for one instalment.

Here: Fuji testnet, test AVAX
