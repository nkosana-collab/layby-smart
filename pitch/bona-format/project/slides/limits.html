What it fixes, and what it doesn’t

It fixes

  • Each payment is credited to the ID it names.
  • Nobody can take the money out by hand.
  • The fee is fixed at creation and capped at 1%.
  • Every payment leaves a public receipt.

−

It doesn’t fix

  • A customer who never collects: the money stays locked.
  • Anyone can act as a shop. No verified registry yet.
  • Moving a deposit out can avoid one lay-by’s fee.
  • Test AVAX only, with a 120-second demo grace period.
  • Not audited. Built for this hackathon.
Backup for tough questions. These limits come from our own audit of the deployed contract. None are hidden from users: the app shows the shop’s wallet on every lay-by and warns that item labels are public.
