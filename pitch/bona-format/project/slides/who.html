Backup · Who can do what

No one can take the money early.

The customer

Pays, cancels while active, moves a deposit at the same shop, confirms collection.

Can’t touch anyone else’s lay-by.

The shop

Creates lay-bys, cancels with a full refund, settles an expiry.

Can’t take instalments before collection.

Another shop

Creates its own lay-bys for the same customer.

Can’t move money between shops.

A stranger

Reads every lay-by and payment.

Can’t pay, cancel or collect.

Us, the deployer

Deployed it once.

Can’t pause, withdraw or change anything.

No owner, no admin, no pause, no upgrade, no withdraw function.

Backup for questions about trust. Each “can’t” is a rule in the deployed contract: only the customer can pay or confirm collection, only the shop can expire, and a deposit can only move between two of the same customer’s lay-bys at the same shop.
