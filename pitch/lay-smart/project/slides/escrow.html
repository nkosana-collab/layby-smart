Where the money waits

Consumer

Pays each instalment into one agreement ID.

Escrow

( Contract balance )

Nobody can withdraw it by hand. Not the shop, not us.

Business

Paid only when the customer confirms collection.

The shop never holds the instalments. They sit in the contract. The shop is paid when the customer confirms they have the item. On a cancellation or expiry the contract splits the money itself: the fee to the shop, the rest back to the customer.
