Hypothesis support

I think this is what’s going to happen because…

When every instalment carries its agreement ID and a public receipt, a wrong-lay-by dispute stops being one person’s word against another’s. Customers check their own balances across shops, and shops can show they never touched the money early.

Variables that may affect the outcome…

  • Customers need a wallet, and must learn to use it.
  • Today it is test AVAX, not rand. Real use needs a regulated way in and out.
  • Shops must accept being paid only on collection.
  • A customer who never confirms collection leaves the money locked.
This is our bet, not a proven result. The variables on the right are the honest risks: onboarding, money in and out, shop adoption, and the one gap in the contract, where funds stay locked if a customer never confirms collection.
