How a lay-by moves

Active

Shop creates it

Completed

Final instalment paid

Collected

Customer confirms · shop paid

Or it ends early:

Cancelled

Customer: fee, rest refunded. Shop: full refund.

Expired

Shop settles after the grace period

Five states, in the exact order the contract stores them. The customer can cancel while the lay-by is active and pays at most the 1% fee. The shop can cancel while it is active or fully paid, and must refund everything. After the completion date plus the grace period, payments stop and the shop can settle the expiry.
