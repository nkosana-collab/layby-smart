Consumer Protection Act

Awareness

Money paid stays the customer’s until delivery.

Any cancellation penalty is capped and shown up front.

An agreement can only end after a grace period.

The refund is everything paid, minus the lawful fee.

Section 62 principles, as the prototype models them. Not legal advice: check the official text.

The CPA already says how lay-by should work. The trouble is that these rules live on paper and depend on the shop following them. Lay-Smart writes them into the contract itself.
