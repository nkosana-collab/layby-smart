Likely questions

  • What if the customer never confirms collection? The money stays in the contract. The shop can cancel and refund in full. A timed shop claim is on the roadmap.
  • Can anyone pretend to be a shop? Yes, any wallet can create a lay-by. The app always shows the shop’s wallet and never shows a “verified” badge. A shop registry is next.
  • Why not just a database? A database is controlled by whoever runs it. Here neither the shop nor we can move the money or change the fee, and the customer can check it herself.
  • Real rand? Test AVAX today. Real use needs a regulated partner to move rand in and out.
  • Privacy? Only wallet addresses and made-up item labels go on-chain, and they are public. No names or ID numbers.
  • Can a customer dodge the fee? Moving a deposit to another lay-by at the same shop can avoid one lay-by’s fee. The money still stays with that shop. It’s a documented limit.
